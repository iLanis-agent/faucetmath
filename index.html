Faucet math

The tap runs while the kettle boils, the drip in the night sounds small, and the aerator packet promises the world in percent. Faucet math turns flow and minutes into honest liters, the drip into a tub count, and the aerator swap into the cut it really is. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Flow plan - tap flow and daily minutes become liters a day and a year.
  • Drip plan - drips a minute become liters, and a labeled tub count.
  • Aerator plan - old flow versus new flow becomes the saved liters and the honest cut.

Labeled norms

Labeled drip volume: about 0.25 ml a drip. Labeled tub: 150 liters. Labeled aerator flows: about 5-6 L/min versus a labeled open tap around 8-12 L/min. These are reference values, not rules - time your own tap with a jug and a stopwatch for the number that matters.
